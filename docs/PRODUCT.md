# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary Persona:** Academy Operators and Branch Managers coordinating day-to-day cricket academy operations at specific training grounds (managing enquiries, scheduling trials, managing batches, logging attendance, tracking fee dues, and collecting payments).
- **Secondary Persona:** Super Admins and Academy Executives requiring top-level visibility across all owned and franchised branches, financial audit logging, user role management, and academy-wide reporting.
- **Tertiary Persona:** On-ground Coaches and Trainers logging student attendance, reviewing batch allocations, and managing training drills.

## Product Purpose

CPA CRM is a specialized academy management and customer relationship platform designed for Cricket Pioneers Academy (CPA). It streamlines the complete student lifecycle—from initial public enquiry or walk-in, through trial scheduling, admission, batch allocation, recurring fee collection, and attendance tracking—eliminating manual paperwork, lost leads, and unaccounted cash collections.

## Positioning

Unlike generic business CRMs or broad education ERPs, CPA CRM is purpose-built for sports academies with multi-branch, franchise-aware operations. It natively provides strict branch data isolation, cricket-specific batch schedules, guardian-student relationships, and cash-drawer reconciliation workflows tailored for busy academy front desks.

## Operating Context

- **Front-desk / Ground Offices:** Fast-paced environment with continuous walk-ins, phone enquiries, and parent interactions during morning/evening training slots.
- **Primary Tool Usage:** Desktop, laptop, and tablet web browsers.
- **Interaction Style:** High-density, fast-entry operational dashboard optimized for quick tabular filtering, immediate status badges, keyboard-friendly data entry, and instant timeline audits.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Enquiry & Pipeline Management:** Lead capture (website forms, walk-ins, calls), automated deduplication, trial booking, structured follow-up reminders, reassignment between staff with immutable timeline audit trails.
- **Student & Batch Directory:** Multi-branch roster management, age-group categorization, guardian contact records, active/inactive enrollment statuses.
- **Attendance Tracking:** Daily session logs, batch roll calls, historical attendance percentages, and coach-level attendance verification.
- **Fee Management & Finance:** Partial payment support, receipt generation, multiple payment modes (Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer), end-of-day cash reconciliation, and strict audit logging for reversals.
- **Strict Multi-Tenant Branch Isolation:** Backend-enforced role-based access control (RBAC). Branch managers and coaches are hard-isolated to their designated branch; only Super Admins possess cross-branch oversight.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** CPA Cricket Academy CRM (Cricket Pioneers Academy)
- **Visual Identity:** Professional, athletic, clean, and utilitarian. Focus on clarity, immediate legibility, high contrast, and rapid operational speed over decorative fluff.
- **System Theme:** Clean corporate light mode with dark sidebar accents and crisp status indicators for academy stages (e.g., New, Trial Scheduled, Admitted, Overdue).

## Product Principles

1. **Zero-Lead Leakage:** Every enquiry and trial must have an explicit owner, a scheduled next action, and an immutable history log.
2. **Strict Branch Security by Default:** Multi-branch boundaries are enforced at the backend and database layer; no operator can view or modify records outside their authorized branch scope.
3. **Desk-Speed Efficiency:** High information density and zero visual clutter. Critical actions (reassignment, logging payment, taking attendance) must be reachable in two clicks or fewer.
4. **Audit Immutability:** Financial entries, attendance records, and lead reassignments must leave an indelible audit footprint for academy management and franchise owners.
